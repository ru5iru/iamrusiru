# Blogger visibility implementation checklist

**Scope: Blogger theme, published posts, Pages and Blogger/search settings only.** This is the checklist for the next fix phase. No theme/content fixes have been applied. The visibility setting changed by the owner has been rechecked publicly.

There are **50 actionable items** below. Checked items are verified complete; unchecked items are proposed fixes, improvements or verification tasks. They are not promises of rankings or AI citations. Existing styling must be preserved; any contrast correction requiring a visual change needs an explicit scope decision. Do not add features that already work merely to increase an SEO score.

Order: **P1** major correctness/evidence work; **P2** editorial/performance/reuse improvement or owner verification; **P3** low-impact cleanup. Types: **Fix** confirmed defect, **Improve** useful enhancement/editorial work, **Verify** evidence/account/editor check still needed. Stable finding references point to [REPORT.md](REPORT.md). The broader 150-control audit is [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md).

## Preserve what already works

Server-readable article bodies, summaries and FAQs; one canonical per core route; native publication/modification fields; author identity; BlogPosting/WebSite/Blog/breadcrumb semantics; discoverable pagination; working image URLs; responsive layouts; mobile menu/Escape; dark persistence and the existing styles. Do not replace microdata, add synthetic AI markup, stuff meta keywords, invent reviews/ratings, or require llms.txt merely to satisfy a generic audit tool.

Primary guidance: [Google generative AI optimization](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [Blogger settings](https://support.google.com/blogger/answer/9691230?hl=en), [Google's supported meta tags](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags). Field results and private owner settings remain verification tasks, not confirmed failures.
